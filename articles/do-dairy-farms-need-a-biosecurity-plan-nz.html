On-farm biosecurity

When a New Zealand dairy farm needs a biosecurity plan

We have not found a New Zealand law that makes every dairy farm hold a written biosecurity plan. Every farmer does have a legal duty to report pests and diseases not normally seen in New Zealand, and Federated Farmers said in 2018 it was adding biosecurity plans to its sharemilking agreements.

MPI's advice to farmers is to write up a biosecurity plan, using one from your industry body or processor if they have one, or DairyNZ's biosecurity planner (MPI).

What the law asks of every farmer

Section 44 of the Biosecurity Act 1993 puts every person under a duty to inform the Ministry, as soon as practicable, of the presence of what appears to be an organism not normally seen or otherwise detected in New Zealand (Biosecurity Act 1993). On a farm that call goes to the MPI exotic pest and disease hotline, 0800 80 99 66. Put the number in your plan, with the name of the person who rings it.

Sharemilkers and farm owners

In June 2018, during the Mycoplasma bovis response, Federated Farmers said its contracts were being updated to include provisions for sharemilkers and farm owners to develop a farm biosecurity plan (Federated Farmers). Check the biosecurity clause in your own agreement. Where both parties have stock or staff on the farm, write the plan together and name who does each job.

Your milk company and industry body

Beef + Lamb New Zealand says a completed copy of its template should meet the biosecurity management plan requirements of councils, processing companies and industry organisations (Beef + Lamb NZ). Ask your milk company whether it has a plan template or wants to see yours.

How many farms have one

An OSPRI survey found about one in three dairy or commercial beef farmers had a biosecurity plan or were working on one. OSPRI published the figure in January 2026 (OSPRI).

For the parts of a plan, see What goes in a dairy farm biosecurity plan. For the steps, see How to write a biosecurity plan for your dairy farm.

Common questions

Do sharemilkers need a biosecurity plan?

Federated Farmers said in June 2018 that its contracts were being updated to include provisions for sharemilkers and farm owners to develop a farm biosecurity plan. Check the biosecurity clause in your own agreement.

What do I have to report?

Section 44 of the Biosecurity Act 1993 puts every person under a duty to inform the Ministry of what appears to be an organism not normally seen or otherwise detected in New Zealand. The MPI exotic pest and disease hotline is 0800 80 99 66.

Where do I get a template?

MPI points farmers to their industry body or processor, or to DairyNZ's biosecurity planner. DairyNZ publishes a free Biosecurity Farm Plan template.
