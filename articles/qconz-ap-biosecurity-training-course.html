The QCONZ AP Biosecurity Training Course

QCONZ delivers the MPI-approved online training for New Zealand Accredited Persons. If your business handles imported sea containers at a Transitional Facility, this is the course you need.

Why QCONZ?

QCONZ is the recognised provider of Accredited Person biosecurity training in New Zealand. Their courses are developed to meet MPI's requirements and are designed specifically for the people doing the work — the APs at Transitional Facilities who inspect and oversee the unpacking of imported sea containers every day.

The training is delivered entirely online, which means no travel, no venue costs, and the flexibility to complete modules when it suits your schedule. This makes it practical for individuals and for businesses training multiple staff across different sites.

MPI Approved

QCONZ's AP training is approved by the Ministry for Primary Industries. Completion of the appropriate course is a required step in the MPI accreditation process for Accredited Persons handling imported sea containers.

What the Course Covers

The full AP Sea Container Course teaches you everything required to safely receive, inspect, and manage imported sea containers at an MPI-approved Transitional Facility. This includes:

  • New Zealand's biosecurity legislative framework and the role of the AP within it
  • How to inspect an imported sea container — what to look for, how to document it
  • How to identify and respond to biosecurity risks found during inspection
  • Supervising the unpacking of containers and managing biosecurity risks during devanning
  • Using the AP Resource Book effectively — the reference guide APs use in the field
  • Record-keeping obligations and MPI reporting requirements

The course uses video demonstrations, practical scenarios, and interactive exercises to make the content accessible and easy to apply on the job.

Who Needs to Complete the Course?

The AP Sea Container Course is required for:

  • First-time APs — anyone applying for MPI Accredited Person status for sea container inspection at a Transitional Facility must complete the full course as part of their accreditation application
  • Existing APs due for renewal — APs must complete the Refresher Course within two years of their previous full course to maintain their accreditation

The free Biosecurity Awareness course is recommended for all staff at Transitional Facilities — not just APs. It builds foundational knowledge across your team and contributes to your business's biosecurity culture.
