Professional Accreditation

Does Your Business Need an Accredited Person?

An Accredited Person (AP) is a qualified individual authorised to carry out specific biosecurity activities on behalf of a business — including inspections, treatments, and clearance procedures.

Many New Zealand businesses in logistics, horticulture, and import/export are required to have an AP on staff. QCONZ offers the recognised AP training programme.

  • Required for many import and export operations
  • Authorised by the Ministry for Primary Industries (MPI)
  • Covers inspection, treatment, and clearance procedures
  • Nationally recognised qualification
